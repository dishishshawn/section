# Section Demo Script (5-7 minutes)

## Opening - The Problem (1 min)

"Every oil & gas land services firm lives the same broken cycle. A client hands you a tract—30, 40, 50 documents scattered across a data room. A senior landman spends two to six weeks parsing those documents by hand: reading instruments, tracing chains of title, computing fractions, abstracting leases. By the time the deliverable ships, your best people have spent weeks on grunt work. And then—everything they built evaporates. The client pays. The project closes. Six months later, a different client wants work in the same county, and you start over from zero.

You already know this is the bottleneck on your business. You can't hire fast enough. The people who can actually do this work take years to train. And meanwhile the work itself is deeply repetitive—the same deeds, the same leases, the same fractional math, over and over."

## The Insight (1 min)

"Here's what we see: everything a firm like Compass sells—ownership reports, runsheets, leasehold confirmations, curative packages, A&D memos—is just a different *view* of the same underlying thing. Documents parsed into a structured model of who owns what, burdened by what, due when.

Today that model lives in one landman's head, an Excel workbook, and a folder nobody will open again. What if it lived in a system?"

## The Product (1 min)

"That system is Section. Every project gets what we call a Land Graph: tracts, instruments, parties, interests, obligations. You drop documents in. Claude extracts. The graph populates. And then every deliverable becomes a view or an export from that single source of truth.

Let me show you."

## Demo (2-3 min)

### Runsheet View
- Click into "Garfield County 640 - STACK Area" project
- Show the Runsheet
  - Chain of title flows top to bottom
  - Gaps and breaks highlighted in red
  - Specific curative needs called out: "No deed from John Smith estate to heirs - need affidavit of heirship"
  - Red flags make it obvious where the chain is broken

### Ownership View
- Switch to "Ownership" tab
- Show fractional math:
  - Compass Operating: 1/2 (50%)
  - Smith Estate: 1/4 (25%)
  - Unknown Heir #1: 1/8 (12.5%)
  - Unknown Heir #2: 1/8 (12.5%)
- Show waterfall visualization
- Show final pie chart
- "One source of truth. Change a single deed, and every number updates everywhere."

### Obligations Calendar
- Switch to "Obligations" tab
- Point out high-priority items in red:
  - Rental payment due in 15 days ($500)
  - Continuous drilling requirement in 45 days
  - Primary term expires in 90 days
- "The system watches your deadlines so your team doesn't have to."

### Risk Dashboard
- Switch to "Risk Dashboard"
- Show portfolio-wide stats:
  - 12 total leases in project
  - 3 expiring soon
  - Royalty range: 3.125% - 18.75%
- Show flagged issues:
  - Title defects (critical)
  - At-risk leases (high)
  - ORRI burdens (medium)
  - Rental payments (low)
- "This is the view that compresses a two-week diligence into an afternoon of senior review."

### Export
- Click "📄 Export Report" button
- PDF generates in seconds
- "Ownership report in Compass's template. Every field auto-populated. Ready to send to the client."

## The Business Case (1 min)

"Three returns on your investment:

**First—internal leverage.** Your team takes on 3-5x the project volume without hiring. A deliverable that took two weeks takes three days. Your senior people spend time on judgment calls, not document-chasing.

**Second—productized services.** You start selling 'Section access' as a subscription to your E&P clients. New recurring revenue line. You stay embedded in their workflow.

**Third—equity upside.** Section sells to any mid-size operator with a land function. That's thousands of potential customers. Your investment buys a stake in that upside. And because you shaped the product, Section is better than what a team without your expertise would ever build."

## The Ask

"We want Compass as a design partner and lead investor. Design partner means your team uses Section on real projects, gives us unvarnished feedback, shapes the roadmap. Investor means you fund the first phase of the build in exchange for equity and favorable long-term terms."

## Why Now

"The methane regulations. The M&A wave. The retiring landman workforce. Maturing Claude-class LLMs. They all converge on one moment: land work is about to get automated. The question is whether Compass defines how, or watches someone else do it. We'd rather do it with you than for you."
